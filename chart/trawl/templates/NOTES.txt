  kubectl port-forward --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} svc/{{ include "trawl.fullname" . }} 5514:{{ .Values.service.port }}

Keep the port-forward running. In another terminal on this machine:

  curl --fail-with-body --insecure https://localhost:5514/api/v1/health

Expect status "ok" and every entry in "checks" to be "ok". HTTP 200 alone can
report degraded dependencies. --insecure is only for this local check.
