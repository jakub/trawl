== verdict

'host=web1 f!=x': parquet filter pushdown control=kept, absent=kept
'host=web1 OR f=x': parquet filter pushdown control=none, absent=kept
  the absent corpus pushes a filter the control does not: DuckDB pushes no
  OR across two columns into the scan, and the absent operand folds to NULL,
  which leaves a one-column filter it can push
projection on control: f projected
projection on absent: f not read: the scan projects only columns the files carry
probe median on control: 4.00 ms (p90 4.00 ms)
probe median on absent: 4.00 ms (p90 4.00 ms)
load average at end: 2.05 2.45 2.65
